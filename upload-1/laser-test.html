How to test

  1. Turn the laser on and switch its Bluetooth on (the Bluetooth symbol shows on its screen).
  2. Tap Connect to the laser: the list shows only Bosch devices, so pick the one there (it may have no name). If the list is empty or full of other things, use Find my laser.
  3. Take a measurement with the laser's own button, or with Fire from here: the first tap turns the laser on to aim, the second takes the reading, just like the laser's button.
  4. Check it against the laser's screen, then tap Copy the log and paste it to Claude.

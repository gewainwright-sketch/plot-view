How to test

  1. Turn the laser on and switch its Bluetooth on (the Bluetooth symbol shows on its screen).
  2. Tap Connect to the laser: the list shows only Bosch devices, so pick the one there (it may have no name). If the list is empty or full of other things, use Find my laser.
  3. Take a measurement with the laser's own button, or with Fire from here: the first tap turns the laser on to aim, the second takes the reading, just like the laser's button.
  4. Check it against the laser's screen, then tap Copy the log and paste it to Claude.

Ask and control

Once connected. These come from Bosch's own protocol document (MT protocol LRF command set 2.5.0, in bosch-docs). Every answer is decoded in the log. The function buttons change the laser's measuring function: watch its screen.

Settings byte=

Write byte reads the 11 settings bytes, changes one, writes them back and reads them again. Byte 4 is backlight mode (0 auto, 1 on, 2 off), 6 is units (2 = metres), 8 is read-only (memory index), 9 and 10 are unknown on this model.

Experimental

Keypad bypass sends the laser's key presses to this page as events and the laser stops reacting to them itself; bypass OFF (or a power cycle) restores it.

Key bit

Command 27 "keypad pattern" exists in Bosch's own app library: a 32-bit pattern of keys. Which bit is which key is unknown, so try bits 0 to 15 one at a time and watch the laser; the On/Off key is the worst case (turn it back on).

Census of command numbers

Read the known answers: asks only the commands the laser has already answered with information (none of them do anything). Tap it, change one thing on the laser (back out to the measuring screen), tap it again: what changed is listed.

Scan from

Scan asks each command number from the one given, with no data, and logs the status it answers: 04 = not on this laser, 05 = locked (Bluetooth cannot unlock it), 06 = exists and wants data, 00 = answered. Results are kept on this device (Show census). It never sends 10 35 3a 3b 45 48 4e 4f 52 54 55 56 or the Bluetooth-module commands e8 to ff, keeps its place so a crash cannot lose it, and pauses if a command answers "done" with no information or the laser goes quiet.

Type any bytes in hex; the checksum is added for you.
